# Privacy policy

Feedline is a free, open-source RSS reader for Android, published on Google Play by Vempus.

**Feedline does not collect, store or share any of your data.** It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and neither Vempus nor the developers run a server that it talks to.

## What stays on your phone

Your feeds, articles, images, reading history, saved articles and settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What the app connects to

Feedline connects directly from your phone to:

- the feeds you subscribe to, and the article pages and images they link to
- a site's address, when you use it to find its feed or icon
- GitHub, only if you choose to follow Feedline updates

As with any web request, those sites can see your IP address and that the request came from Feedline. Their own privacy policies apply. Links you open go to your browser.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to fetch feeds, and to hold image downloads for Wi-Fi if you ask it to.
- **Run in the background and start at boot:** only for the optional background refresh, which is off by default.
- **Vibration:** for small touch feedback.

## Children

Feedline is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
